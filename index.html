How I work

Approach & philosophy

"We only win as a team."

I'm a servant leader — I typically lead from the back, coaching teams towards solutions rather than prescribing answers. But I shift to directive mode when situations demand clarity and speed. Context determines approach.

I believe in continuous improvement through marginal gains (Kaizen) whilst recognising when radical change (Kaikaku) is necessary to reach sustainable progress. I break ambitious targets into incremental, data-backed steps.

Breaking down barriers between engineering, product, design, and business isn't a platitude — it's the actual mechanism through which value gets unlocked. Silos are a delivery problem, not a personality problem.

Radical ownership Teams that inform rather than ask permission. Scalable, resilient organisations are built on trust and accountability — not process and approval chains.
Transparency I call out problems when I see them, own mistakes openly, and assume best intent. Psychological safety isn't a programme — it's a daily practice.
Marginal gains Consistent, measurable progress beats disruptive transformation. Sustainable improvement comes from building good habits and eliminating friction, not big-bang rewrites.
Disagree & commit Healthy debate is a signal of a functioning team. Once a direction is set, everyone rows in the same direction — with full accountability for the outcome.
Genuine curiosity My profession is my hobby. I bring the same energy to understanding a new domain, technology, or team as I do to solving the problem at hand.
